01 About

I'm an applied scientist focused on natural language processing, with a soft spot for the messy gap between a model that works in a notebook and one that holds up under real users, real latency budgets, and real languages.

Most of my recent work sits at the intersection of large language models and production constraints: training and aligning domain-specific LLMs (including DialpadGPT), squeezing them down with post-training quantization, and evaluating how well they actually reason over messy, multi-turn conversational data — in English, Spanish, French, and other languages.

I'm based out of Toronto, Ontario, Canada.
